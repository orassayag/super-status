# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith and
working with less context than you have.

## What that means here

**Expected:** focus on the technical question; accept that a maintainer with
limited time may close or decline something reasonable; give a reproducer rather
than a verdict; disagree about the work, not about the person.

**Not accepted:** harassment or personal attacks; sexualised language or
imagery; publishing someone's private information; sustained disruption of
issues or pull requests; discrimination on any basis.

## Scope

Applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Reports are handled confidentially and on a
best-effort timescale — see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Responses range from a
private word through comment removal to a permanent block, at the maintainer's
discretion and in proportion to what happened.

This is a small personal project with one maintainer, so there is no appeals
body. If you think a decision was wrong, say so once, plainly.
