This is a single-maintainer project. See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for what that
means in practice, and [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md) for the release checklist.

Decisions that change rendered output are weighed against one rule: a statusline
is read in passing, many times an hour, so a field earns its place by being
something you would act on. Anything else belongs behind a default-off flag.
